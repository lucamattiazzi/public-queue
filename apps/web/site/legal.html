Terms of service

Public Queue provides a hosted job relay connecting a frontend to inference software on your own computer. You supply and operate your hardware, models, network connection and compatible runtime, and remain responsible for their licenses and use. This release supports asynchronous text inference, including encrypted progressive text, without tool execution.

Free has no subscription charge. Premium costs €29 per year, including applicable taxes, and renews annually unless cancelled before renewal. Checkout shows the amount and billing details before payment. Cancel renewal and manage invoices through the dashboard’s billing portal. Cancellation at period end preserves Premium access until the paid period expires. Deleting your account cancels active subscriptions immediately and removes access.

The limits displayed on the pricing page apply per account. One account provides one workspace. Jobs may expire, run again after an interrupted attempt, or fail when a device, model, network or service is unavailable. There is no guarantee of exactly-once inference and no uptime SLA. Do not use this service for safety-critical workloads. Export needed results before their retention period ends.

Keep credentials and encryption keys private. Do not expose other people’s inference endpoints, submit unlawful material, evade account limits, resell shared access without agreement, or interfere with the service. We may restrict access to address abuse or security incidents. You can contact the operator to contest a restriction or request help.

Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. If applicable law grants a withdrawal or refund right, contact the operator using the address above; this text does not exclude it. Other refund requests are reviewed by the operator. The applicable law and competent courts follow mandatory rules protecting consumers. Material subscription changes will be communicated before renewal, with the option to cancel.

Privacy notice

The operator identified above is responsible for personal data used to provide this service. We store your email, account and project identifiers, terms acceptance version, authentication sessions, device names and public keys, hashed access credentials, plan, billing references, usage counts and job routing metadata. Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses and operational metadata. Application logging does not intentionally record prompt bodies or access tokens.

For E2E encrypted jobs, prompts, model names, progressive text blocks and responses are encrypted on your device or in your browser. The relay sees ciphertext, sizes, timing, status, identifiers and routing. Your browser’s private key stays in browser storage and the agent’s private key stays on your computer. Plaintext jobs, if explicitly enabled on a self-hosted project, do not have this protection. Encryption cannot protect against compromised devices or a modified frontend: independently host reviewed frontend code for that threat model.

This deployment uses Plausible, hosted at check.grokked.it, to measure page visits for jobboard.grokked.it. We do not configure analytics events containing prompts, model responses or access credentials.

Processing necessary to supply the requested service and handle payments supports the contractual relationship. Abuse prevention and operational security serve legitimate interests; legally required billing records are retained to meet legal obligations. There are no advertising cookies in this application. Essential session cookies expire after 30 days; login challenges expire after 15 minutes.

Job payloads are erased on completion and replaced by the result; terminal results are retained for up to one day on Free or seven days on Premium. Pending jobs expire at their deadline (24 hours by default, up to seven days on Premium). Private operator accounts with an Owner exemption retain terminal results until account deletion rather than automatic age-based removal. Daily usage counters are kept for up to 63 days. Authentication links are removed within roughly one day, expired sessions during maintenance. Account data is kept until deletion. Webhook event identifiers may be retained to prevent duplicate processing. Encrypted backup copies are rotated within 30 days when the documented deployment policy is followed.

The configured hosting provider, SMTP email provider and Stripe process the data needed for hosting, sign-in messages and payment respectively. Card details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout and are not stored by this application. Stripe may retain financial records after account deletion. The actual providers, hosting region and international transfer safeguards for this deployment are described below.

You may export account metadata and delete your account from the dashboard. Use the SDK to retrieve and decrypt job results before deletion. Contact the operator for access, correction, deletion, portability, objections or other applicable data rights, and for requests involving retained backups or billing records. You may complain to the competent data protection authority.
