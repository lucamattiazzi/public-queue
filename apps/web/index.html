Your models, beyond localhost.

A small bridge between your frontend and the AI models running on your computer. Jobs wait in a persistent queue; your computer picks them up when it is online.

No open ports at home. Prompts and responses are encrypted between your browser and your local agent.

Open the console →

How it works

  1. Install the agent on the computer running your models.
  2. Pair it in the console and connect your frontend with the SDK.
  3. Send a job. Follow the response as it arrives, or come back for it later.

For Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, oMLX, vLLM and compatible text inference servers. You bring the models and the compute.

Get started

  • Console — connect a device and try a prompt.
  • Local agent — runs on your computer, with Node.js 24+.
  • Browser SDK — connect your own frontend.
  • Source code — MIT licensed, with setup guides.

Currently a personal beta. Console access requires an owner key; public signup and paid plans are not open yet.
